Our Story & Journey

Founded in 2025, Global Carry Transportation started with a clear vision: to revolutionize freight shipping by making it transparent, highly dependable, and cost-effective. Already, we manage a nationwide fleet and hazmat logistics capabilities, serving clients from small local businesses to major corporations across the United States, Canada, and Australia.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence by investing in cutting-edge tracking technology, expanding our specialized OTR capabilities to include Step Deck, Flatbed, and Conestoga logistics, and most importantly, partnering with professional drivers who prioritize safety above all else. We handle your logistics securely so you can focus on growing your business.
